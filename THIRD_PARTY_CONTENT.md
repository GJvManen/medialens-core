# Third-Party Content and Source Data

MediaLens Core may reference third-party media sources, IPTV/FAST feeds, channel metadata, platform routes, broadcaster pages and external playback URLs.

These references are treated as route metadata only. MediaLens Core does not claim ownership of third-party content and does not guarantee that any source is available, authorized, complete, accurate, current or playable in all locations.

## Source Categories

MediaLens Core can organize sources into categories such as:

- official broadcaster pages;
- official platform routes;
- IPTV/FAST streams;
- public media metadata;
- external watch routes;
- user-provided source lists.

## Responsibilities

Anyone deploying MediaLens Core should review configured sources before making them available to users. This includes checking:

- rights and authorization;
- geographic restrictions;
- provider terms of service;
- stream availability;
- accuracy of metadata;
- use of names, logos and trademarks;
- local laws and regulations.

## No Endorsement

The presence of a third-party source, channel, brand, platform or stream route in MediaLens Core does not imply endorsement, affiliation or partnership.
